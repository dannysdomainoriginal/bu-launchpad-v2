What it does

An intelligent companion designed specifically for college students. It automatically parses syllabus PDFs, syncs with your university calendar, and generates personalized active-recall flashcards from lecture audio recordings. No startup jargon—just a tool to keep you from failing your midterms.

Why we built this

"Two months ago, I completely missed an online quiz worth 15% of my grade because it was buried on page 4 of our outdated school portal. I realized my classmates were struggling with the same organizational chaos. I built this assistant not to replace studying, but to automate the stressful admin work of being a student so we can actually focus on learning."
